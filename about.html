Our story

Built on one Dzire and a straight answer on price

Samriddhi Tours & Travels began in Varanasi in 2014 with a single Swift Dzire, a landline and a rule we still follow: quote the real fare, then stick to it. Word spread through the guests we drove — pilgrims who found their darshan planned better than they expected, and families who were not surprised by the bill at the end.

Over a decade later we run a mixed fleet of sedans, SUVs, Innovas, Cruisers and tempo travellers, along with a small team of drivers who have spent years on the Varanasi–Ayodhya–Prayagraj–Bodhgaya circuit. Most of our work still comes from repeat guests and their referrals, which is the only review that has ever mattered to us.

We are a local business, not a call centre. When you ring the number on this page, you reach someone who knows which gate at Kashi Vishwanath is open, how early to leave for the 6:45 PM aarti, and where a 26-seater can actually park in Godowlia.

What guides us

Six things we refuse to compromise on

Honest pricing

One fare, agreed in writing before the trip. Tolls and parking at actuals, with receipts. We have never run a hidden surcharge.

Driver quality

Commercially licensed, police-verified drivers who speak Hindi and workable English, dress neatly and know the routes cold.

Vehicle upkeep

Serviced on schedule, cleaned before every trip, AC checked, tyres and brakes inspected before long outstation runs.

Punctuality

The car reaches your pickup point before time, not at it. Flights and trains are tracked so delays cost you nothing.

Respect for guests

Elderly passengers, women travelling alone and families with children set the pace of the day. No rushing, no forced shopping stops.

Reachable, always

Two numbers, both answered round the clock. If plans change mid-trip, one call is enough to reshuffle the day.
