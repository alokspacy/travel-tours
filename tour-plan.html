How outstation fares work

No mystery in the maths

Total kilometres travelled × the per-km rate for your vehicle, with a minimum of 250 km billed per day, plus the driver night allowance (₹300 per night for cars, ₹500 for tempo travellers). Toll, parking and inter-state permits are added at actuals with receipts. We work the whole figure out with you before you confirm.
